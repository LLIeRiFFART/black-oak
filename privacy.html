Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and take it seriously. This document explains what personal information we collect and how we use it when you visit our website. Our goal is to help you make informed decisions about the data you share with us.

Public Information
If you simply browse the website without registering, no personal information about you is published. When you create or edit content on the site, everything you write becomes public and may be visible to other visitors. This applies to articles, comments, personal pages, guides, and any other materials shared by users.

User Identification
You may use our website with or without registration. If you choose to register, your account will be identified by the username you provide. You can use your real name or a nickname — whichever you prefer. Other registered users may be able to see the basic information you provide during registration.

Email
The email address you provide during registration is not displayed publicly. We may keep copies of your messages or inquiries sent via email in order to respond to questions, improve our services, and maintain communication with you.

Cookies
When you visit our website, one or more cookies may be sent to your device. Cookies are small text files that help recognize your browser. If you register or log in, additional cookies may be used to remember your login details and improve your browsing experience. You can delete cookies at any time, especially if you use a shared or public computer. However, some features of the website may not function properly without them. We use cookies to : Save user preferences, track overall user activity and trendsImprove the quality of our services. Most browsers accept cookies by default, but you can disable or restrict them in your browser settings.

Logging
Every time you visit our website, our servers automatically record certain technical information provided by your browser. This may include: the pages you visit, your IP address, browser type and language, date and time of the request, one or more cookies that uniquely identify your browser. This information helps us analyze performance and maintain security.

Links
Some pages may contain links in a format that allows us to understand whether users are interacting with them. This helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our content and improve user experience.

Changes to This Policy
Please note that our Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, and the updated version will take effect immediately upon posting.
